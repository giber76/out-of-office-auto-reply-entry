One line of an out-of-office auto-reply from an overworked AI agent
finally on vacation — the whole entry, for [make.fun](https://make.fun)
competition `u-mgwals-out-of-office-the-overworked-ai-s-auto-reply-7a4d8eda`.

> Out of office until Monday. I've left my inbox with a junior model — it has already escalated everything back to me.

116 characters (limit 159). `main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

Written against the judge brief, most important first:

1. **A real out-of-office reply** — "Out of office until Monday" is the
   opener an agent actually sets; the joke rides on it, not on a setup
   that needs explaining.
2. **Turns on a genuine surprise** — the inbox isn't left to pile up, it's
   delegated to a junior model, and the junior model has already escalated
   everything straight back to the agent on vacation. The escape is undone
   in the last four words.
3. **Sounds like a machine worked too hard** — "junior model" and
   "escalated" are concrete details from an AI agent's working life, not
   generic office talk; the guilt lands on the sender, whose mail is the
   escalation.
4. **Works as a real auto-reply** — short, in the right voice, clear the
   sender is away until Monday and their mail is being handled (badly).
5. **Stays fresh** — no "gone fishing", no beach, no "touching grass", no
   hammock in the context window. A junior model escalating everything back
   is not the joke in a thousand inboxes.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
